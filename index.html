Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Compassion & Change

Queens of Change Foundation is an Indian Government registered NGO dedicated to creating a world where every woman and girl can live with dignity, access education, and break free from the barriers that hold them back.

From menstrual health education to skill-building programs, we work at the grassroots level — listening, learning, and lifting communities together across India.

🌺

Women Empowerment

Building confidence, independence, and leadership in every woman we reach.

🩸

Menstrual Health

Breaking taboos and ensuring access to sanitary products and health education.

📚

Education for All

Supporting girls to stay in school, learn, and dream without limits.

What We Do

Our Core Initiatives

Our multi-faceted approach tackles menstrual health, education, and women's empowerment across India's communities.

🌸

Sanitary Pad Distribution

Providing free sanitary pads to underprivileged girls and women, ensuring menstrual health and dignity for all.

📚

Health Education

Breaking stigma through comprehensive education about menstrual health, hygiene, and reproductive rights.

👑

Women Empowerment

Skilling programs and mentorship to help women become self-reliant and confident leaders in their communities.

🤝

Community Outreach

Direct engagement with marginalized communities to understand and address their specific needs and challenges.

💼

Skill Development

Vocational training and digital literacy equipping women with skills for employment and entrepreneurship.

🎓

Education Support

Scholarships and study materials ensuring girls never have to choose between education and circumstances.
